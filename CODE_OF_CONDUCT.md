# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in GreenBubbles a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users;
- protecting the privacy and safety of people whose communications may be
  affected by the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit
  permission;
- requesting, sharing, or pressuring someone to share private conversations,
  credentials, account data, or identifying artifacts;
- using the project to enable stalking, coercive surveillance, account
  takeover, or access to data without the owner's authorization;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that are inconsistent with this Code of Conduct, and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns privately through
[the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/bojieli). Do not include
private user data in the initial contact. For a product vulnerability, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters. Enforcement
decisions will follow the
[Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html#enforcement-guidelines).

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.
